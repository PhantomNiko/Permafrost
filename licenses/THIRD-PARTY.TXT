Permafrost 1.0 component scope

The current firmware and WinterLua are in-house implementations. The current
build does not include OpenBIOS or upstream Lua.

The embedded Mach distribution is retained unchanged and is excluded from
reverse engineering. Its supplied notices remain in the image's recovery
volume under license/Mach-Kernel-LICENSE.txt, license/Mach-Kernel-README.txt
and license/Acks.md. Pooka's Free Software License does not replace these terms.

Historical milestones using external components retain their licenses and
notices. Their sources are obtained from:
https://github.com/PhantomNiko/openbios
https://github.com/PhantomNiko/lua/tree/Permafrost

See LICENSES.md and DEPENDENCIES.md for source and distribution scope.
